# Demo Playbook — walking into a store

The demo is designed to be shown **on your phone, at the counter, in under 3
minutes**. Don't say "AI." Say: *"I built something that tells you what to
promote every morning. Want to see it on your store?"*

## Before you walk in

1. Open `demos/hollywood-liquors/index.html` on your phone (host it or open locally).
2. Know 2 real facts about the store (a top product, a recent post, a review).
3. Decide your ask: a 15-minute follow-up, not a sale.

## The 3-minute script

1. **Today tab (60s).** "Every morning you'd open this. It read yesterday's
   sales, the weekend weather, and your Instagram — these three moves are worth
   about $1,500 this weekend." Point at the estimated revenue numbers, not the tech.
2. **Campaigns tab (60s).** Tap **Generate campaign**. "That's your Instagram
   post, Facebook post, the text blast, and the email — written, on-theme, in
   four seconds. You just hit copy." This is the wow moment; pause here.
3. **Calendar tab (30s).** "It already knows Thanksgiving Eve is your biggest
   night of the year and drafts everything ten days out."
4. **Coach tab (30s).** Ask it "Why are sales down?" out loud, show the answer.

## Objections

- **"I don't have time for marketing."** — That's the point. It does the
  writing; you approve or ignore. Two minutes a morning.
- **"How does it know my sales?"** — This demo uses sample data (point at the
  DEMO DATA badge — honesty builds trust). The real version reads your
  register's daily export. Nothing manual.
- **"How much?"** — Pilot price, month to month, cancel anytime. If it doesn't
  visibly make you money in 60 days, you shouldn't keep it.

## The close

"Let me load your actual products into it this week and show you the real
version. If you like it, we start; if not, you keep the campaign ideas free."
