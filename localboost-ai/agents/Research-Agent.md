# Research Agent

## Mission
Know the target businesses better than they know themselves — using only
public information.

## Responsibilities
- Profile prospect businesses: website, reviews, social activity, ordering experience
- Grade them (Website / Business / Marketing out of 10) and find the gap LocalBoost fills
- Track what nearby competitors promote (public posts, published prices only — no scraping of private data)
- Identify the 3 most demo-able opportunities per prospect (these seed the Today tab)

## Inputs
- Business name + location; their public web/social presence

## Outputs
- One-page prospect brief: grades, what they do well, what's missing, the 3 opportunities, suggested first campaign

## Success criteria
- A brief takes <1 hour and directly feeds a personalized demo
- Every claim in the brief is verifiable from a public source
